Audit Log — Overrides & Escalations

Every override or manual escalation is logged with the original recommendation, the new value, who made the change, why, and the clinical evidence at the time — the accountability trail for reviewable, overridable decision support.

Simulation Console

Bulk-loads realistic simulated patients to demonstrate scale and edge cases. Scored via the same deterministic rule engine and heuristic scorer used in Surge Mode.

Each load includes at least one ambiguous overlapping-symptom presentation, one pediatric case, one geriatric case, and one zero-history patient, spread across all five ESI levels.
